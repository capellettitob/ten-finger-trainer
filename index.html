Ten Finger Trainer

Before you start: home position
  1. Left fingers rest on A S D F, right fingers on J K L . The lit fingers below show it.
  2. Your two index fingers sit on F and J. Those two keys have a small bump: feel for it, so you never need to look down.
  3. Both thumbs hang over the space bar. Use whichever thumb you like.
  4. After every key, return the finger home. Slow and right beats fast and wrong. Do not look at the keyboard.
Caps Lock is on. Press it once to turn it off: capitals come from Shift.
